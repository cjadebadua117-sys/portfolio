Selected work

Two systems, built end to end

Both are Django applications running on MySQL via XAMPP, with a shared glassmorphism design language — models, migrations, roles and screens all written by me.

01

BHIMS

Boarding House Information Management System

A boarding house listing and booking system for students near campus. Landlords post listings with photos, rates and amenities; admins moderate every listing before it goes public; students filter by barangay and price, message landlords in a thread, and get confirmed bookings.

StudentRegister, browse & filter listings by barangay, price and amenities, send inquiries, chat with landlords, receive confirmed bookings.
LandlordManage boarding houses and rooms — photos, rates, capacity, amenities — reply to inquiries and accept students.
AdminApprove or reject listings, manage landlords, students and barangays, review reports, view analytics, edit site settings.

02

CIS-Prox

Student Presence & Real-Time Locating System

A campus attendance and presence system. Students sign in and out of rooms and laboratories, classmates see who is currently where, and instructors get dashboards for class attendance, reports and broadcasts. Privacy controls let students decide who can see them, and a campus Wi-Fi gate keeps check-ins tied to the actual campus network.

StudentRegister, enroll in a section, sign in and out of rooms, track activity hours, manage privacy and find classmates.
InstructorClass attendance dashboards, Flag Raising Ceremony records, laboratory history, broadcasts to sections or rooms.
AdminUser, instructor and system management, reports, cleaning schedules and an auto-logout middleware for shared consoles.

About

Backend, database and the screen people touch

I like building the whole loop, not just the database part: the models and migrations, the role-based views that guard them, and the interface that makes the rules obvious instead of confusing.

Both systems sit on a stack schools already run — Python, Django and MySQL over XAMPP — which keeps them maintainable by whoever picks them up next.

On the front end I build my own design system rather than leaning entirely on a framework: shared colour, glass and motion tokens, keyboard-visible focus states, and reduced-motion support, so every screen in a system feels like the same product.

How I work

  • Roles and permissions designed before the first view
  • Migrations and seed data so a fresh install is usable
  • Automated tests, including adversarial cases
  • System diagrams: context, DFD, ERD and flowchart
  • Real screenshots and honest empty states
